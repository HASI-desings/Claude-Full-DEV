# Continuous Creative Innovation (applies to every phase)

Never get trapped in conventional website patterns. At each gate, ask at least three of these and act on the best answer:

1. What has this industry never seen on a website?
2. What if we approached the company's identity from a completely different perspective?
3. Can an ordinary object become the central visual identity?
4. Can the site communicate through movement instead of relying on text?
5. Can navigation become part of the experience?
6. Can the two objects influence *how users interact*, not just how it looks?
7. What single moment will visitors remember and describe to someone else?
8. Can the actual product or service become an interactive experience on the page?
9. Can physical-world behaviour (weight, friction, magnetism, growth, wear) inspire a digital interaction?
10. What is the most unexpected **but meaningful** way to communicate the company's purpose?

Look to architecture, nature, physics, engineering, mechanical systems, art, industrial design, cinema, photography, science and everyday objects.

**Challenge the first idea.** For any important decision, write your first idea, then force two alternatives that differ in kind (not degree), then choose — and say why the winner beat the first idea, or why the first idea survived.

The goal is not strangeness. It's something genuinely distinctive that makes sense for this company. Unconventional, but understandable, usable, accessible and commercially effective.
